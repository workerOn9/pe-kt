# Base-10 Diophantine Reciprocal

Consider the diophantine equation 1/a + 1/b = p/10^n with a, b, p positive integers and n a positive integer. For n = 1 there are exactly 20 solutions:

| a | b | p |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 10000000000 | 10000000000 |
| 2 | 5000000000 | 7500000000 |
| 4 | 2500000000 | 6250000000 |
| 5 | 2000000000 | 7200000000 |
| ... | ... | ... |
... (full table in problem)

How many solutions are there for 1 ≤ n ≤ 9?
